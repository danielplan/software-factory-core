# What's new in Kobble

What each version of Kobble changed for you, in plain words. New versions
arrive by themselves. Written by Kobble's makers at each release.

## 0.14.0 (2026-11-22)

- New: **Releases of your products.** A Releases page turns what was merged into a numbered release, with notes written for you; the kobold suggests the number only when you press Prepare.
- New: **What's new in Kobble.** Settings › Updates says what each version changed, in your language.
- New: **Prototypes in every state, and dig deeper.** Each variant can be shown empty, loading, with a problem or with many items, switched by chips under it. Pick one variant to refine it with new ones.
- Changed: **Built from the prototype you agreed.** A prototype on the board is handed off only once you agree on it and its variant, and the factory builds from its screens, keeping their look.
- Changed: **A shorter hand-off.** The card says what you planned and decided, without more; Doodle shows which step it is at while it writes it up.
- Changed: **One library per job.** Each kind of product names the library it uses for each job, and the factory checks new work keeps to them.
- Changed: **The builder asks instead of going on and on.** After three build sessions on one card it stops with a draft pull request and asks you to split the card or let it go on.
- Fixed: **The builder repairs its working copy** when its link to git breaks, keeping the old folder.
- Fixed: **Resolve stays resolved.** A card sent back to settle a clash with main no longer asks for Resolve again while the builder works on it.
- Fixed: **The prototype maker remembers.** It goes on in the same conversation about a prototype, also after Kobble restarts.

## 0.13.2 (2026-11-21)

- New: **Send feedback from anywhere.** Ask Sprocket or choose "Send feedback" and tell the makers what is wrong or what you wish for, in one window. Afterwards you can see whether it was seen, planned or done.
- Changed: **Your knowledge stays up to date with the code.** Before a change goes up for review, the builder makes sure the notes about the product were looked at too.
- Fixed: **Italian and German everywhere.** Words that were still only in English now speak your language, screen reader labels included.
- Fixed: **No more blank page** when Kobble was rebuilt while the page was open. It loads itself again.
