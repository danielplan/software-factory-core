# No planning stage: a card in To do is the go-ahead

[Knowledge base](../index.md) · [Decisions](index.md)

## Context and Problem Statement

The factory had a Planning stage, with a planner kobold and a board of its
own. An idea was thought through with the team in an exploration, and then
planned again on its card before the builder could start. Where should the
thinking happen, and what tells the builder to begin?

## Considered Options

- Keep a Planning stage: a planner writes a plan on the card, the team
  agrees it, then the card moves on.
- No planning stage: thinking happens in explorations, and a card in To do
  is the go-ahead, built as written.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: no planning stage. Thinking happens in explorations, with Doodle and
the team; the hand-off writes the card, and putting it in To do is the
go-ahead. The board's Backlog holds ideas that wait in Explorations. The
planner and the factory's own board are gone; the stages are Backlog, To
do, In progress, Review and Done.

### Consequences

- Good, because there is one place to think, and the team sees one step
  from an agreed idea to a build.
- Good, because a card made by Fix on a signal, or by a person, is built as
  it is.
- Bad, because a card written by hand without an exploration gets no plan
  beyond its own text; the builder asks when it is unclear.
